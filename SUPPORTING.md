# Supporting AI Use Assessment

AI Use Assessment is one of five open-source apps for setting and evidencing AI governance, released by Triodian Pty Ltd under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE). Boards, counsel and regulators need a shared, inspectable way to do this work, so anyone can read, run and improve the apps. Help in whatever way suits your role.

There are four ways to help. Pick the one that fits; none needs the others.

## 1. Use it

*For developers, and anyone who runs the app.*

Run the app, open an [issue](../../issues) when something is unclear or wrong, and send fixes as pull requests. Starring the repository helps others find it. Contributors are credited in the usual way, through the commit history and GitHub's contributor list.

**Sign your commits.** Add a [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) sign-off to each commit (`git commit -s`). It confirms you have the right to submit the change. Under section 5 of the Apache License, a contribution is made under the same licence.

## 2. Review it

*For AI lawyers, regulatory lawyers and others who know the field.*

Review one part of the method: the evidence ladder, the obtainability test or a review point, which takes about an hour. Tell us whether a finding drawn this way would stand up with a reviewer or supervisor. Email [info@triodian.com](mailto:info@triodian.com?subject=Reviewing%20AI%20Use%20Assessment) with "Review" in the subject, or open an issue if you are happy to be seen.

- **Anonymous or credited, your choice.** We credit reviewers only with their written consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.
- **Review is not advice.** A reviewer gives no legal advice, forms no client relationship, gives no view on any organisation's obligations and takes no responsibility for how anyone uses the app.
- **Current or recent regulator staff** can review anonymously only, and are never listed.
- **Written comments that change the method text or records** are contributions too. We will ask you to confirm they are made under the Apache License, as with code.

## 3. Tell us you use it

*For internal counsel and organisations, including regulated entities.*

Tell us privately that your organisation uses or is trialling the app, and what would make it more useful. Email [info@triodian.com](mailto:info@triodian.com?subject=Using%20AI%20Use%20Assessment) with "Using" in the subject.

**Private by default.** We never name a person or an organisation without written approval from both, and either can withdraw it.

## 4. Fund it

*For organisations that rely on the app.*

Organisations can fund the apps' upkeep by invoice. Email [info@triodian.com](mailto:info@triodian.com?subject=Funding%20the%20open-source%20apps) with "Funding" in the subject.

- **What it pays for:** maintenance, security fixes and keeping the Obligations Atlas current. We report how funds were used once a year.
- **What it does not buy:** influence over the method or its outputs.
- **What it is not:** a fee for Triodian's facilitation or deployments, which are separate.

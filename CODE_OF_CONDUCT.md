# Code of conduct

We want the Triodian projects to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, whatever their background or experience.
We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be kind and considerate, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the people who use the apps.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or any other
project space.

To report a problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's **Security** tab and say it is a
conduct report. The maintainers will review it, keep it confidential, and respond as the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe.
